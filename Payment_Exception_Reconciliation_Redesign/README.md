# Payment Exception & Reconciliation Workflow Redesign

> **Enterprise Business Analysis Case Study**
>
> A comprehensive Business Analysis project focused on redesigning the payment exception handling and reconciliation workflow for a financial institution. The project aims to improve reconciliation efficiency, reduce manual intervention, enhance exception visibility, and establish standardized business processes through structured requirements engineering and process optimization.

---

# Executive Summary

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analysis engagement focused on optimizing payment exception management and reconciliation operations.

The objective was to analyze the existing reconciliation workflow, identify operational bottlenecks, redesign the business process, define business and functional requirements, improve stakeholder collaboration, and establish complete requirements traceability.

The repository contains the core deliverables typically produced during a Business Analysis engagement for workflow transformation initiatives.

---

# Business Problem

Financial institutions processing high volumes of transactions often face challenges such as:

- Delayed reconciliation cycles
- Manual exception handling
- High operational workload
- Lack of standardized reconciliation procedures
- Poor visibility into unresolved payment exceptions
- Increased operational risk due to inconsistent processes

These issues negatively impact operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, and customer satisfaction.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aimed to:

- Standardize the reconciliation workflow
- Reduce manual exception handling
- Improve payment exception visibility
- Increase reconciliation accuracy
- Improve stakeholder collaboration
- Establish clear business and functional requirements
- Improve requirements traceability throughout the project lifecycle
