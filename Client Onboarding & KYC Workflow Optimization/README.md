# Client Onboarding & KYC Workflow Optimization

> **Enterprise Business Analysis Case Study**
>
> A comprehensive Business Analysis project focused on optimizing the client onboarding journey by redesigning the Know Your Customer (KYC) workflow, improving operational efficiency, reducing onboarding delays, and enabling data-driven decision making through SQL, Python, and Power BI.

---

# Executive Summary

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analysis engagement for improving a financial institution's client onboarding process.

The objective was to analyze the existing onboarding workflow, identify operational bottlenecks, redesign the business process, define business requirements, establish traceability, and deliver analytical insights through interactive dashboards.

The repository contains all project deliverables typically produced during a Business Analysis engagement.

---

# Business Problem

Financial institutions often experience:

- Lengthy onboarding cycles
- Manual KYC verification
- High customer drop-off rates
- Limited visibility into onboarding performance
- Inconsistent stakeholder communication

These challenges reduce operational efficiency and negatively impact customer experience.

---

# Business Objectives

The project aimed to:

- Improve onboarding efficiency
- Reduce customer drop-offs
- Standardize KYC workflows
- Improve stakeholder visibility
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Establish clear business requirements
- Improve requirements traceability
